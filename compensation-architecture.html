Work example 02 of 04 · Compensation + workforce planning

Compensation architecture + workforce forecast

A 31-person professional-services firm. Three hires approved. One offer above a tenured peer. Merit in six weeks. Pay had been set one offer at a time, and nobody could say what the year would cost.

Context
31 people · one delivery track shown here · founder approves all pay
Layer
Compensation architecture · Workforce planning
Built
Levels · ranges · placement · merit rules · hiring plan · payroll forecast · scenarios
$6K

Key findingcompression between a pending L3 offer and a tenured L3 with broader scope.

Salary ranges are not the point. The point is knowing what every people decision does to the forecast.

01

One model, eleven links

Each link answers a question the next one depends on. Break one and the budget number at the end is a guess.

  1. RoleWhat is this job?
  2. LevelHow much scope does it carry?
  3. RangeWhat does that scope pay?
  4. PlacementWhere is each person in it?
  5. CompressionWho is paid out of order?
  6. MeritWhat does performance move?
  7. PromotionWhen does someone change level?
  8. Planned hiringWho joins, when, at what?
  9. Current-year payrollWhat will this year cost?
  10. Exit run-rateWhat does next year start at?
  11. People budgetDoes it fit?

05

Merit and promotion rules

Merit depends on performance and where the person already sits in the range. Written down once, applied to everyone.

Merit increase · April cycle
PerformanceBelow midAt midAbove mid
Exceeds5.0%4.0%3.0%
Meets4.0%3.0%2.0%
Developing1.5%0%0%
  • Pool checkWeighted across current ratings: 3.5% of eligible payroll. Matches budget.
  • PromotionMoves to at least the new level’s low. Typically 8–16%. Modeled from its effective date.
  • OffersHiring zone by default. Above mid needs leadership sign-off and a compression check first.

08

Who owns what, after

The model only works if someone keeps it current. That was agreed before handoff.

Leadership decides

  • Range approval, once a year
  • Any offer above mid
  • Compression corrections
  • Which scenario to run

Operations runs

  • Every offer checked against the zone
  • Merit calculated from the matrix
  • Forecast updated monthly and on every change
  • Headroom reported with the monthly close
