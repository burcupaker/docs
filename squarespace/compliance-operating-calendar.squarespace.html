Compliance operating calendar

A 42-person company in five states. The operations director inherited a folder of checklists and, a month later, a payroll vendor change. Nobody could say who owned what, or how they would know it had been done.

Context
42 people · 5 states (A–E) · new payroll vendor · no in-house HR lead
Layer
Compliance operating system · Employee lifecycle
Built
Operating register · owner map · re-run triggers · recurring calendar · confirmation log
9

Key findingrecurring compliance checks had no named internal owner.
