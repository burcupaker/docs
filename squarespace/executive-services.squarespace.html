The deeper diagnosis

Nobody delegates the first hour. Then it’s ten.

Each task takes five minutes, so it never seems worth handing off. Added up, they take the week.

  1. Month 1

    Scheduling your own meetings. It’s quicker than explaining.

    True.
  2. Month 6

    Answering every email yourself, to keep things moving.

    Mostly true.
  3. Year 1

    Collecting board materials, the week before, every quarter.

    Manageable.
  4. Year 2

    Booking travel, chasing receipts, renewing software.

    Small things.
  5. Now

    About a quarter of the week goes to work with no decision in it. The important projects get what’s left.

The fix isn’t better time management. It’s handing the logistics to someone who owns them.
