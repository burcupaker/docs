The deeper diagnosis

The company grew. The CEO’s thinking partners didn’t.

At five people, the founders decide everything together. At thirty, the hardest calls get made alone.

  1. 5 people

    Co-founders decide over lunch. Everyone knows why.

    It works.
  2. 15 people

    The first managers arrive. They bring problems, not options.

    Manageable.
  3. 25 people

    A senior hire doesn’t work out. Nobody questioned the role first.

    Expensive.
  4. 35 people

    The board asks for a plan. It’s built in a week.

    Survivable.
  5. Now

    The hardest calls — who to hire, who to let go, what to stop — get made late, alone and without the full picture.

The fix isn’t another advisor on the phone. It’s someone inside the work who helps you decide, then makes sure it happens.

What Operationary does

A right hand for the decisions only you can make.

We work alongside the founder or CEO as a senior operator: framing decisions, bringing in the facts and the people who need a say, and carrying the decision through to the team. The judgment stays yours.

Recommendation: Option B

The problem isn’t this person’s effort. The role now holds client relationships and delivery operations for 30 people. They’re strong at the first and stretched on the second.

Replacing them risks the two biggest accounts in order to fix the half of the job that a second hire would fix sooner.
